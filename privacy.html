Privacy Policy

DatumAnomaly Uploader · last updated 10 November 2026

Who uses this app

DatumAnomaly Uploader is used only by the owner of the DatumAnomaly YouTube channel, to manage that channel. It is not a public service and it has no other users.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel and to set their thumbnails, titles, descriptions, translations and subtitle tracks. It does not read, collect or store data about viewers or any other Google user.

How data is stored

The OAuth credentials are stored privately in the owner's own working environment and are used only to perform the uploads described above. The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and it does not use Google user data for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the DatumAnomaly channel page: youtube.com/@datumanomaly.
